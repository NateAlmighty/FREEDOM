The Four Pillars of Total Preservation

Most beginners stick to one method because the others seem intimidating. This guide demystifies all four, giving you a modular toolkit for any crop:

  • Canning: Locking high-acid foods (tomatoes, jams) and low-acid foods (beans, meats) in sterile vacuum seals for shelf-life up to 5 years.
  • Fermenting: Using salt and time to create probiotic-rich krauts and pickles without heat or electricity.
  • Drying: Removing moisture to halt decay, turning herbs, fruits, and jerky into lightweight, calorie-dense staples.
  • Freezing: Flash-freezing blanched vegetables to retain texture and nutrients for immediate use later in the season.

The catch? Most online advice is fragmented. This manual consolidates current USDA and National Center for Home Food Preservation (NCHFP) guidelines into a single, executable protocol.
